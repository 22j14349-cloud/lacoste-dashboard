Method notes

How to read the badges and estimates

Reported
Stated by Lacoste, a named executive, a regulatory filing or the company's own reports.
Estimate
Derived here from reported figures. Example: 2023 sales ≈ €3.0bn ÷ 1.08 ≈ €2.75bn, because 2024 grew 8%.
2019 and 2021 sales. The CEO said 2021 was "back to 2019 levels", and 2022 sales of €2.5bn were +26%. So 2021 ≈ €2.5bn ÷ 1.26 ≈ €2.0bn, and 2019 is about the same.
Conflicts. Wikipedia lists €2.9bn for 2023; trade press says "close to €3bn" for 2024. The €4bn target has been dated 2026, 2027 and 2028–30 over time — the dashboard uses the latest (2028–30). A 2011 figure of €1.6bn covers licensees' sales, so it isn't on the same basis as later numbers.
Currency. US-dollar revenues are converted at 1 EUR = 1.15 USD, an approximate 2025–26 average.
Illustrative charts. Growth scenarios and climate pathways are straight compounding or straight lines to stated targets — not forecasts or reported progress.
Chart colours were checked with a colour-vision-deficiency validator. Every chart also has a Table view.
